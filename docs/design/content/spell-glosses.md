# Spell gloss drafts — the institutional register

**Status:** drafts, not authored content. Generated against the voice established by the
shipped voice-line banks and the two existing glosses (*The Mended Decade*, *The Empty Room*),
then reviewed. W20 places them; nothing here is in `node.json` yet.

The register is **institutional comedy played completely straight** — grant applications,
tenure, peer review, the endowment, faculty politics conducted through magic that can unmake a
mind. Nobody in the world finds any of it funny. That is the joke.

The test every gloss must pass: **the mechanic stays legible.** The dwarf bark about
cross-referencing against fire is funny *and* is a load-bearing description of redundancy
against library loss. Comedy that obscures the rule is a failure.

Here are the 25 spell glosses, followed by the five rejected drafts. The comedy is kept strictly institutional, played completely straight, with the mechanical function made legible in every entry.

# Spell Glosses

**The Renewed Appointment** (Creo Corpus)
Rebuilds the body's native vitality without altering the mind, which is fortunate, as the mind holds the grants. An outpatient procedure, assuming the patient can still operate the door latch.

**The Endowed Chair** (Creo Vim)
Tethers a tenured mage's failing breath to the ambient magical reservoir, effectively outsourcing their heartbeat to the endowment. The faculty strictly outlives the buildings. The administration considers this an acceptable depreciation curve.

**The Deferred Sabbatical** (Muto Corpus)
Alters the target's biological clock to run strictly on academic time, which is to say, not at all. Useful for preserving irreplaceable faculty during budget reviews. Wake them when the funding clears.

**The Vellum Audit** (Intellego Herbam)
A diagnostic scan of all organic material within the stacks, used to assess what is readable and what is actively decomposing. Also flags unauthorized substitutions of sheepskin for calfskin. The copying backlog does not survive close inspection.

**The Duplicate Hand** (Rego Herbam)
Directs parchment to ink itself from a master copy, halving the scribe's labor without technically replacing them. The master copy must be intact, which is rarely the case. Vellum is expensive, but so is tenure, and only one of them writes.

**The Indexed Ash** (Perdo Herbam)
Reduces the physical text to clean soot while leaving its indexing data perfectly intact, ensuring the catalogue remains accurate even when the shelf is empty. Used primarily for space management and the quiet disposal of heresies. The archivists consider it good practice.

**The Reallocated Office** (Perdo Mentem)
Carefully unmakes the portion of the mind responsible for a given concept, leaving the subject otherwise intact and functional. Standard procedure for anyone who reads the endowment's real ledger. The subject usually takes up a new hobby.

**The Revised Transcript** (Muto Mentem)
Rewrites the target's memory of an event, adjusting the facts to align with official faculty minutes. The process leaves no scar, only a vague sense that the semester went well. No one ever remembers the original draft.

**The Silent Committee** (Rego Mentem)
Holds the subject's will in a closed loop, permitting all thought but forbidding any disclosure of a designated topic. The Ethics Board uses it on themselves before retreats. It is considered a courtesy.

**The Drafted Threshold** (Rego Limen)
Aligns the spatial coordinates of an unanchored breach, forcing it to open onto a stable, surveyed location rather than whatever is currently adjacent. The facilities team requests three weeks' notice. They rarely get it.

**The Bricked Window** (Perdo Limen)
Unmakes the structural integrity of a dimensional seam, collapsing the breach into ordinary, solid space. Maintenance uses it when the rent on the other side comes due. The closing makes a sound like a book shutting, but louder.

**The Study Candle** (Creo Ignem)
Conjures a small, manageable flame that the professor assured us cannot burn down the hall. It usually doesn't, provided you don't breathe on it. I think I missed the part where we make it stop.

**The Walking Puddle** (Rego Aquam)
Supposed to lift water from the basin and move it to the beaker. It moves the basin instead. The professor says understanding why is on the exam.

**The Foundation Survey** (Intellego Terram)
Determines the exact load-bearing capacity of underlying stone and soil, identifying fault lines and empty chambers. A prerequisite for any new construction, and entirely ignored when deadlines loom. The resulting cracks are usually classified as features.

**The Familiar's Reshape** (Muto Animal)
Alters the superficial morphology of a laboratory animal to suit a researcher's spatial requirements, usually making it smaller. The creature retains its original temperament, which is often indignant. The ethics committee does not approve, but they do sign.

**The Grounded Current** (Perdo Vim)
Strips the raw magical charge from an object or area, returning it to a mundane state. Used to clear unstable enchantments before they cascade. It is the magical equivalent of draining the cistern, and just as undignified.

**The Pressured Dome** (Creo Auram)
Generates a localized pocket of breathable, climate-controlled air around a designated area. Essential for fieldwork, and for keeping the draught out of the reading room. The accounting office insists it is cheaper than fixing the windows.

**The True Index** (Intellego Nomen)
Peels back the superficial labels of an object to reveal its fundamental, unalterable designation. Useful for bypassing wards, or for discovering who actually wrote the paper. The true name is always less flattering than the published one.

**The Vested Appearance** (Muto Imaginem)
Alters the perceived visual qualities of a target, making threadbare robes look like full regalia. Does nothing to the actual structural integrity of the garment. Entirely standard for graduation ceremonies.

**The Deferred Deadline** (Rego Fatum)
Imposes a structural delay on an imminent, predetermined event, pushing the outcome forward on the timeline. It does not stop the event, merely reschedules it. The administration uses it on retirement parties and audits.

**The Clean Slate** (Perdo Umbra)
Dissolves the shadow-matter residue left by heavy magical use, erasing the forensic evidence of what was cast. Does not affect the physical damage, only the proof of who caused it. Standard post-exam cleanup.

**The Quick Growth** (Creo Herbam)
Forces the immediate maturation of plant life, bypassing the natural seasons entirely. Used to grow rare reagents in laboratory window boxes. The botanical department files this under 'agriculture' for tax purposes.

**The Soft Stone** (Muto Terram)
Renders solid masonry temporarily fluid, allowing for rapid architectural renovation without demolishing the wall. The stone sets in exactly the shape it is poured, which is rarely perfectly level. The masons hate it, which is how you know it works.

**The Peer Review** (Intellego Mentem)
Allows the caster to read the immediate, conscious thoughts of a subject, usually to verify a claim. The spell does not provide context, only the raw transcript of their internal monologue. It is deeply invasive and used constantly.

**The Inverted Flow** (Muto Vim)
Reverses the fundamental polarity of an active spell, turning a ward into a trap or a healing light into a scouring beam. Theoretically elegant, practically a disaster. Faculty must file a variance before casting it, which they do after.

# Rejected Glosses

**The Infinite Coffee** (Creo Aquam)
*Summons a hot, caffeinated beverage directly into the caster's stomach. Who needs sleep when you have grant deadlines?*
*Why it didn't work:* Modern slang and anachronism; 'caffeinated beverage' breaks the procedural fantasy setting.

**The Talking Door** (Rego Limen)
*The portal now gives directions, but it is always wrong. Campus tours have been a nightmare since we installed it.*
*Why it didn't work:* Too whimsical; anthropomorphizing the portal breaks the deadpan institutional tone and turns the magic into a cartoon.

**The Bad Grade** (Perdo Mentem)
*Inflicts the target with terrible memories of failing exams. The student will cry. We do not care.*
*Why it didn't work:* Too edgy and winking; it tells the reader it is a joke at the student's expense rather than playing the horror straight.

**The Big Hand** (Rego Vim)
*Smites your enemies with a giant spectral fist from above. The Dean prefers we use it for roofing repairs.*
*Why it didn't work:* A generic fantasy trope that clashes with the university administration register; the punchline relies on a weak pivot.

**The Perfect Knot** (Rego Herbam)
*Ties the thread of fate into an administrative knot. No one gets promoted, but no one gets fired either.*
*Why it didn't work:* The metaphor is muddy; the reader cannot tell if this is a staff buff, a debuff, or a literal rope spell, obscuring the mechanic entirely.
