# Mētis: the first authoring pass

**Status:** first pass, content-authored. 29 of 300 nodes, 6 of the 51 v1 nodes.
**Applies to:** `packages/content/data/node.json`, field `knowledgeKind`.
**Change of record:** `openspec/changes/metis-knowledge` (held at proposal depth until after 0.5.0).
**Vision:** §5 (knowledge is physical and mortal), §13 (the open question this answers in part).

---

## 0. Why this document exists

`metis-knowledge`'s proposal rules that a node's `knowledgeKind` is
*"content-authored, not derived, so a cell's deep end is an authoring decision rather than an
automatic consequence of tier."* Vision §13 asks *"how much of the grid is mētis?"* and answers
that the count *"is a tuning output, not an up-front decision."*

Both are true. Neither produces a number. Somebody has to make the first pass, and the pass has to
be defensible from the content that already exists rather than sprinkled to a ratio — because
marking a node mētis is a strong claim. It says **this knowledge dies with people**: it can never
be scribed, it exists only in a mind or a palace, it moves only by teaching from a living holder,
it leaves the universe when its last holder dies untaught, and it cannot be looted because there is
no book to take.

This document is the reasoning. It is written so that a reader can disagree with call number seven
specifically, rather than with the pass generally.

## 1. The principle

**The discriminating question, asked of every node's gloss:**

> Could a competent stranger with the prerequisites reproduce this from a correct written
> procedure?

- If the node's content is a **result** — a procedure, a derivation, a name, a formula, a made
  thing — then a text carries it. **Episteme.**
- If the node's content is a **judgement** — reading a situation, telling apart two things a text
  can only name, a feel for a material or a body, the timing of a moment — so that writing it down
  yields a *description of the ability* rather than the ability, then it is **mētis**.

Four probes make that question operational. The first three argue for mētis; the last two argue
against it, and are the reason the pass did not swallow every Intellego node in the file.

### Probe 1 — Discrimination

Does the working consist of telling apart two things a text can only name?

> `ih-pick-out-the-simple` — *"know it apart from the four things in the same hedge that will kill
> the patient instead. **Most of herb-lore is the apart-from.**"*

A herbal names the plant. The apart-from is what the herbal cannot carry, and the gloss says so in
seven words. **Mētis.**

### Probe 2 — Calibration

Is the value of the working *being right more often than not*, in a way only repetition supplies?

> `ic-take-the-pulse` — *"Every physician thinks she can already do this; **the ones who can are the
> ones who stop being surprised.**"*

The written version of a calibrated judgement is a list of cases, and the ability is exactly in the
cases not listed. **Mētis.**

### Probe 3 — Restraint

Does the discipline consist of *refusing* the obvious improvement?

> `iig-scry-the-coals` — *"What returns is not a picture but a pressure, and **the discipline is
> entirely in refusing to improve it into a picture on the way to the page.**"*

A text can carry a rule. It cannot carry a refusal, and this gloss is literally about what happens
to the knowledge on its way to being written down. **Mētis** — the strongest single call in the
file.

### Probe 4 — The artefact (argues for episteme)

Does the working leave behind a thing that persists and works — a wall, a name, a catalogue, a
bound working, a portal? Then the procedure is what matters, and the artefact is the proof it was
followed. `rt-the-vaulted-hall`, `in-catalogue-of-names`, `rv-the-working-kept-in-iron`,
`rl-open-the-portal`: all episteme, and the vaulted hall is standing there to say so.

### Probe 5 — The literature (argues for episteme)

Does the gloss cite a literature, a treatise, a syllabus, a published working, engraved plates, a
catalogue? That is the content's own testimony that it has been written down and stayed written.

> `cf-the-given-destiny` — *"**Every surviving instruction** opens with the same warning."*
> `mv-the-common-root` — *"Every scholar who has **published** on it has published the same warning,
> in the same words."*

This probe overruled the discrimination probe more than once, and it is why the pass has a defined
shape rather than an aesthetic one.

### The unfalsifiability corollary

A special case of probe 2, needed for Fatum, where several workings succeed invisibly. The question
is **not** "can an observer prove it worked?" but **"can the caster tell, from the working itself,
whether she did it right?"** If not, she can only be told by someone who already holds it — which is
mētis's transmission model exactly.

It cuts cleanly. `cf-make-the-small-luck` (*"quantities too small to prove"*) and `if-cast-the-lots`
(*"every student's first result is indistinguishable from luck"*) are mētis.
`pf-take-the-luck` and `pf-where-nothing-turns-out` are episteme: their targets are named, and the
decline is watchable.

### What mētis is *not*

**Mētis is not secrecy.** `cv-the-made-vis` — *"the four universities that hold it have each
independently decided not to publish"* — is episteme. The text exists and is withheld. A withheld
text can be stolen, leaked, and rediscovered from a fragment; a mētis node cannot, and conflating
the two would make the theft rules incoherent.

**Mētis is not a mortal effect.** `cim-the-perfect-forgery` — *"it stops doing either on the day the
mage who cast it dies"* — is episteme. Its *effect* dies with its caster. Its *knowledge* does not,
and the field is about the knowledge.

### The all-mētis cell

**`intellego-corpus` is 4 of 4, and it is the only one.** Take the Pulse, Sift the Humours, Listen
at the Ribs, Tell the Hour of a Body.

**Intended.** The cell is medicine, and every one of its four glosses is written about a
discrimination made at a bedside: what the body is *currently* doing about itself; what is wrong
against what is merely unusual; which sound is *new*; how long this body has, *"which is why it can
be argued with."* Nothing in the cell is a formula.

The consequence is severe and is the point: **no library in any universe holds medicine.** A
physician-mage teaches an apprentice or the art leaves with her. A raid that kills the infirmary
takes the infirmary permanently, and a fire in the stacks takes nothing from it. This is the
strongest single design statement in the pass and the one most worth arguing with — see hard call 2.

It costs nothing measurable today: `intellego-corpus` is not a v1 cell.

## 3. Structural consequences a reviewer should see before agreeing

These are not side effects. They are what the marking *does*, and they are the reason to read the
list rather than the count.

**A. Fourteen of twenty-nine mētis nodes are tier 1, and mētis roots gate episteme branches.**
There are 22 prerequisite edges from a mētis node to an episteme descendant. Twelve of the fourteen
Intellego cells now have a mētis root. So a universe can hold a *complete written record* of
Intellego Terram and still be unable to reach `it-survey-the-strata`, because the only path runs
through `it-taste-the-soil` and the last person who could taste soil is dead.

That is the mechanic biting hard, and it is meant to. But it is a real fragility, and if the harness
later shows universes collapsing to zero Intellego, the tier-1 roots are the first place to look.

**B. Two of those edges cross out of Intellego.**
`il-sense-the-seam` (mētis) gates `cl-the-made-door` (Creo Limen, tier 3) and `iu-look-along-the-shadow`
(Intellego Umbra, tier 3). Losing a v1 knack therefore closes a non-v1 Creo node. Worth knowing
before anyone assumes mētis loss is contained within a cell.

**C. `in-hear-the-name-spoken` (mētis) gates `im-open-the-locked-room` — the first theft vector.**
Under the proposal, mētis is reachable *only* by reading a mind. This marking makes the mind-reading
node itself downstream of a mētis prerequisite. A universe that loses Hear the Name Spoken cannot
develop the theft vector without paying rediscovery first. That is an interesting, arguably good
loop — and it is not something the proposal anticipated. Flagged rather than smoothed away.

**D. Both named theft vectors are episteme, deliberately.**
`im-open-the-locked-room` and `rn-compel-by-name` are the two cells vision §5 gates theft behind, and
the proposal says mētis "is reachable only by reading a mind, which concentrates its vulnerability
in *Intellego Mentem* and *Rego Nomen*." If those vectors were themselves mētis, the only way to
steal unwritable knowledge would be unwritable knowledge, and the vulnerability the proposal
describes would be self-undermining. Not a rule imposed on the data — a consistency check the data
happened to pass.

**E. `rm-the-shared-mind` — the teaching working — is episteme, and must be.**
*"Hold teacher and student in one attention, so that the lesson does not have to survive the trip
between them."* This is the node that carries mētis. Marking it mētis would make the transmission of
transmission itself dependent on a living holder, which is a bootstrapping trap with no exit.

**F. `rl-open-the-portal` and all of Rego Limen are episteme.**
`contracts.md` §8 requires the cell in every v1 build. A universe that could lose the portal to a
succession failure could lose access to the multiverse in peacetime, with no enemy and no fire. That
would be a different game.

## 4. The ten hardest calls

The valuable part. Each names the node, the call, the argument against it, and why it went the way
it did.

### 1. `iig-judge-the-kiln` is mētis; `iig-the-colour-of-ready-iron`, one tier deeper in the same cell, is episteme

> **t1, mētis:** *"Know the state of a fire without opening the door to it, which is the only way to
> know the state of a fire."*
> **t2, episteme:** *"Smiths have always read heat by colour and always argued about it. **This
> settles the argument**, and a smithy that stops arguing produces roughly a third more."*

**Against:** they are adjacent nodes in one cell, both about reading heat, and the second requires
the first. Splitting them looks like hair-splitting.

**Why it stands:** the second gloss says explicitly that the working *settles* something smiths have
always argued about. Settling an argument is what episteme *is* — it converts a contested reading
into a shareable result, and the payoff is quoted as a number (a third more output), which is a
thing you can only quote once the reading is determinate. The first is inference from outside signs
about a thing you destroy by measuring; the second is the measurement.

This pair is the pass's proof that tier is not the axis, and I would defend it before any other.

### 2. `intellego-corpus` — all four, the only all-mētis cell

**Against:** it blinds every library in every universe to medicine, permanently, in a game where
libraries are the compounding engine. And the fourth node, `ic-tell-the-hour-of-a-body`, returns a
*duration* — a number, which is the shape of a result.

**Why it stands:** the fourth node's own gloss undercuts the number — *"this reads only the hour the
flesh is already walking toward, **which is why it can be argued with**."* An arguable output is an
opinion formed from evidence, i.e. a prognosis, i.e. judgement. Marking it episteme purely to avoid
an all-mētis cell would be marking to a quota, which is the one thing this pass was not allowed to
do.

**The honest counter:** if a reviewer wants one episteme node in this cell, `ic-tell-the-hour-of-a-body`
is the right one to move, and moving it costs the design statement rather than breaking it.

### 3. `cf-the-fated-hand` — mētis

> *"A mage who cannot be struck is not shielded. The arrow turns out to have been aimed somewhere
> else the whole time, and the archer will say so under oath."*

**Against:** it is a tier-3 combat defence with a definite effect. Combat effects are usually
results, and a defence you cannot verify is a defence nobody would fund.

**Why it stands:** the unfalsifiability corollary. The archer sincerely believes he never aimed at
you; there is no event to observe, and so no way for a reader working from a text to learn whether
he has performed the working or merely not been shot at. Instruction requires someone who already
holds it and can watch you. That is mētis's transmission model, arrived at from the gloss rather
than from the cell.

**Weakest point:** this argument, pushed, would take much of Creo Fatum. It does not, because
`cf-the-promised-year` and `cf-write-the-ending` have observable outcomes and
`cf-the-given-destiny`'s instructions demonstrably survive.

### 4. `pn-the-nameless` — mētis, and the only reason Perdo has any in v1

> *"Hold your own unnaming indefinitely. Cheap to maintain, **ruinous to explain to an heir**, and
> the reason some archmages have no biography."*

**Against:** the *technique* of unnaming is general and Perdo Nomen's three shallower nodes are all
episteme. Nothing about the operation resists description.

**Why it stands:** the gloss is a succession-failure story before the mechanic existed. Teaching it
means disclosing your own unnaming to a student — which is precisely the exposure the working was
built to prevent — so it transmits only at real cost, from a living holder, to one person at a time.
And a mage who has removed herself from the record leaves no record for a scribe to copy. Both
halves of mētis's transmission model are already in the sentence.

**Weakest point:** "ruinous to explain to an heir" can be read as *expensive* rather than
*unwritable*, and expensive-to-teach is a `teachCost`, not a `knowledgeKind`. I read the biography
clause as settling it. A reviewer might not.

### 5. `in-hear-the-name-spoken` — mētis, at the root of the most writable cell in the game

> *"Notice that a thing has a name at all, which most people go their whole lives without doing."*

**Against:** Nomen is the form the whole grid writes things down *with*. Its other three nodes are
about spelling a name so a stranger can copy it, deriving one from first principles, and building a
catalogue that outlives its makers. Putting a mētis gate at the bottom of that ladder means a
library holding all of Intellego Nomen is unusable without a living holder of the knack — and it
gates the first theft vector too (§3C).

**Why it stands:** it confers no name and no method. It is the bare perceptual shift on which the
writable work stands, and *"most people go their whole lives without doing"* is the same shape as
`iv-feel-the-air-go-tight`'s *"the ones who never feel it are usually the ones who die indoors"* —
a capacity some people simply never acquire, which is not a thing a text withholds.

**This is the call most likely to be wrong**, and it is wrong in the most expensive direction if it
is. The cheap fix is one word in `node.json`. I did not take it because I could not construct an
argument for it that was not "this is inconvenient."

### 6. `il-stand-in-the-doorway` — mētis, and the weakest of the six v1 calls

> *"Hold the seam itself. A mage who is neither inside nor out is difficult to look at directly, and
> harder still to remember having seen."*

**Against:** it is Intellego, and the previous node in the cell — `il-read-the-binding`, which
returns three determinate forensic facts — is episteme, so "Intellego is suspicious" is not doing
the work here. The working could be read as a straightforward stance with a straightforward
concealment effect.

**Why it stands:** it is not a query. It is a sustained embodied posture whose only evidence is
other people's failure to notice you, which means a student cannot self-check and a text has no
success criterion to state. Compare `rau-step-on-the-air`, marked episteme, where the caster either
stands or falls immediately and unambiguously — that is what a self-checkable embodied working looks
like, and this is not it.

### 7. `iau-reckon-the-season` — mētis

> *"Take a whole year's weather in advance, **coarsely, and be right about it more often than not**.
> Coarse and usually right is worth more to a granary than precise and occasional."*

**Against:** the second sentence describes a *product* — something a granary buys and plans against.
Products are episteme. And the sibling `iau-gauge-the-winds-road`, marked episteme, is also a
forecast that things are scheduled against.

**Why it stands:** the calibration probe at its sharpest. The working's entire stated value is a hit
rate, and a hit rate is the one thing a text cannot confer — a reader who follows the procedure
correctly has no way to know he is right more often than not until he has been wrong for years. The
wind's road, by contrast, is a *measurement* of a wind that is up there whether you read it or not.
The difference is between reading an instrument and having a nose.

**Weakest point:** the granary is planning against it, which implies a track record someone has
written down. That is an argument I can feel the force of.

### 8. `if-the-omen-and-the-coincidence` — mētis, against its own gloss's word "method"

> *"Separate the sign that means something from the enormous number of things that merely happened.
> This is the entire discipline, and the reason Fatum scholars are **so tiresome about method**."*

**Against:** the gloss names method explicitly, and probe 5 says a gloss that cites its own
methodology is testifying to writability.

**Why it stands:** I read the tiresomeness as the *symptom*, not the content. A discipline becomes
insufferable about method when the real skill will not write down and the method is the best
available substitute for it. The first sentence is discrimination under noise in its purest form,
and *"this is the entire discipline"* says the discrimination is the node.

**Weakest point:** this is a reading of tone. It is the least literal argument in the document, and
the one a reasonable person is most likely to reject on the grounds that it reads meaning into a
joke.

### 9. `cau-turn-the-wind` — episteme, despite saying it is not written about

> *"The most-cast working in any university that owns farmland, and **the least written about**."*

**Against:** the gloss appears to say, in five words, that this working is not in the literature.
That is nearly a definition of mētis, and I marked it episteme anyway.

**Why it stands:** *least written about* is a claim about attention, not about writability. It is
Creo — the technique of making a result — and the result is a wind blowing where the mill wants it,
which is its own proof that the procedure was followed. The joke is that the most useful working in
the discipline is too mundane to publish on, not that it resists publication.

**Recorded as a gloss wish in §6:** now that the field exists, this sentence carries a signal it did
not mean to carry, and a reader coming to it cold will make the same first pass I did.

### 10. Rego is 0 of 62, and Muto is 0 of 60

**Against:** two entire techniques, 122 nodes, not one mētis among them. That is either a real
structural finding or a failure of attention, and from outside it looks the same.

**Why it stands:** it was derived, not imposed, and both techniques have a reason.

**Rego is the technique of specification.** Every one of its glosses states its terms — the thing
named, the order given, the duration held. *"Compel a threshold to keep meaning what it means."*
*"Treat two thresholds as the same threshold for exactly one stride."* A compulsion whose terms
could not be stated would not be a compulsion. The technique cannot be mētis without ceasing to be
Rego.

**Muto is the technique of exchange, and every gloss states its conservation law.** *"Nothing is
gained; something is exchanged, which is the entire cell in one sentence."* *"The translation costs
about a third of the working, in both directions, every time."* *"The working improves a thing by
one step and has never once been made to take two."* Those are specifications. Some are literally
numbers.

**The two most arguable members**, examined and left episteme:

- `rt-set-the-stone` — *"Put a block exactly where it belongs, on the first attempt, without a
  crane."* The mason's eye is real, but this is the tier-1 root of the cell that is vision §4's
  worked example of *magic as a build-rate number*, and the next node up says so in as many words.
- `pt-pull-down-the-arch` — *"Unmake **the one stone the rest were argued into leaning on**."*
  Finding the keystone under fire is a sapper's craft. But the grid already has a separate node for
  the seeing — `it-read-the-fault`, *"See where the ground intends to fail"* — so the Perdo node can
  rely on it rather than contain it. Where the grid separates the seeing from the doing, the seeing
  is where the mētis goes.

**And the counter-evidence that Intellego was not made a rule either:** `intellego-aquam` is 0 of 4.
Sounding a well, assaying a pool, tracing a current, fathoming a watershed — all measurements, all
returning determinate facts, all episteme. Four other Intellego cells are 1 of 4.

## 5. What was checked, and what it cost

- **`rediscoveryMultiplier` was not touched.** The proposal makes mētis costlier to rediscover
  *"expressed through the existing `rediscoveryMultiplier`, not a new mechanism"* — but bumping
  magnitudes is a tuning act, and every magnitude in the file is `tuningStatus: untuned` and belongs
  to the balance harness. The authoring invariant the change's task 7.3 asks for was checked
  instead: **no mētis node is cheaper to rediscover than an episteme node of the same cell and
  tier.** It holds with no edits, and `shipped-content.test.ts` now asserts it so a future authoring
  pass cannot break it silently. When the mechanic is implemented, raising those multipliers is a
  measured decision and not this document's.
- **No gloss was changed.** Wishes are recorded in §6 instead.
- **`contentRevision` moved twice**, once per commit:
  `6b18886a…` → `d97caaaa…` (field added, `episteme` on all 300)
  → `5c319f82…` (twenty-nine judged `metis`).
- **No metric moved.** Before regeneration, all three balance gates reported `baseline-invalid` on
  the content hash and `pass` on every metric with **delta exactly 0.00000** — thirty metrics across
  the three sweeps, not one moving by a representable amount. The baselines were regenerated once,
  after the content was final, and `regenerate-baseline.mjs` printed `no metric moved.` for all
  three. Nothing in the rules path reads `knowledgeKind`: `metis-knowledge` is held at proposal
  depth until after 0.5.0, so this is authored data with no consumer, and the baseline diff is
  provenance in the strict sense.
- **The golden replay fixtures were not regenerated**, because no simulated result changed. The full
  suite (3,783 tests, 265 files) passes.

## 6. What the glosses made me want to change — reported, not changed

### Gloss wishes

1. **`cau-turn-the-wind`** — *"and the least written about"* now reads, in a file that has a
   `knowledgeKind` field, as a claim about writability rather than about attention. It is not meant
   as one (hard call 9). A phrase like *"and the least remarked on"* would keep the joke and remove
   the false signal.
2. **`iau-reckon-the-season`** — if this is meant to be episteme, *"coarse and usually right"* needs
   a companion clause naming a table or a rule. As written it is a hit rate, and hit rates are
   calibration (hard call 7).
3. **`il-count-the-doors`** — *"the seams nobody built on purpose"* pulls toward mētis inside a
   gloss that is otherwise a census. If episteme is intended, that clause is the one doing the
   pulling.
4. **`ic-tell-the-hour-of-a-body`** — the one node whose reclassification would end the all-mētis
   cell, and its *"which is why it can be argued with"* is what makes it mētis. If the cell should
   not be all-mētis, this is the sentence to revise, not the marking.

### Three findings for `metis-knowledge` itself, which are not gloss problems

1. **There is a third mind-reading vector, and the proposal does not name it.**
   `mm-translate-the-expert` (Muto Mentem, tier 3): *"Change another scholar's knowledge into the
   shape your own mind keeps things in. She keeps hers. What you carry off is a translation, and
   translations lose things. **Universities that count two theft vectors are counting the two that
   are prosecutable.**"* The proposal says mētis *"is reachable only by reading a mind, which
   concentrates its vulnerability in Intellego Mentem and Rego Nomen."* This node is a third route,
   it is mind-to-mind — the only channel mētis travels — and the content already flags that it is
   the unprosecutable one. The spec should either name it or say why it does not apply.

2. **`pim-unmake-the-demonstration` is a working that converts episteme into mētis.**
   (Perdo Imaginem, tier 3): *"The working survives. The picture of it does not, and after one
   generation those are the same thing."* If the design ever wants an in-game way to *make*
   knowledge unwritable — a weapon aimed at an opponent's library rather than at its books — this
   node already describes it.

3. **`mv-the-second-tradition` is the per-working version of the Art of Memory override.**
   (Muto Vim, tier 4): *"a memorised formula rewritten as a room in a palace. The translation costs
   about a third of the working, in both directions, every time."* The proposal restates the Art of
   Memory's `store` hook as "all knowledge in this universe is mētis." This node prices the same
   conversion for a single working, in both directions, and the `magic-traditions` delta should
   probably reconcile with it.

   Related: `paq-unwrite` (*"Take the water out of ink"*) is the standard raid tool against a library
   that cannot be carried or burned — and it does nothing whatever to mētis. That asymmetry is worth
   stating in the change's `design.md` as a defensive property of mētis rather than leaving it to be
   rediscovered during implementation.

## 7. How to disagree with this document

Every call is one word in `packages/content/data/node.json`. The list of twenty-nine is in §2 and
§4; nothing about the pass is spread across code.

If the harness later shows something — universes collapsing to zero Intellego, or mētis never being
lost at all because teaching is cheap — the fix is a value edit and a `contentRevision` bump, and
this document should gain a section saying what moved and why. Vision §13 is right that the count is
ultimately a tuning output. This is the first pass, made from the content, so that there is
something specific for the measurement to disagree with.
