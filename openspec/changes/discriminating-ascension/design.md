## Context

`god-agency` (0.7.0) specifies ascension as a declared terminal condition behind two disjoint
paths, and `packages/coordination/src/god/ascension.ts` implements them. The machine-meta probe
(`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-08-11-ascension-meta-findings.md`) found that a bot which never acts
reaches the same state as the only bot that wins. This design decides what the win condition should
read instead, and it is written against measurements taken from this build rather than from the
argument that produced the constants.

Constraints that bind every option below:

- Determinism, fixed-point only in the rules path, content in validated data files (`CLAUDE.md`).
- Vision §8a: prestige must not compound without bound; the condition must be *reachable but not
  routine*; *defeat is not the opposite of ascension*.
- `god-constant.json` authors a retune order and calls `ascension-tier-gate` the first knob.
- Golden replay fixtures are never regenerated by this campaign.

## How the measurements were taken

The reference universe (`@mm/scenario`) was built at `runSeed 0x00090001`, `cohortSize 12`,
`foundingMages 1`, `foundingNodes 6`, and stepped directly with `step(state, actions, rng)`, actions
screened through `@mm/agent-api`'s `admit` so that only legal submissions reached the core — the same
gate a session uses. God state was read from the `god-state` component. Four policies, one action
per tick:

| policy | what it submits |
|---|---|
| `passive` | nothing (the `passive-control` equivalent) |
| `breadth` | `permitTechnique` / `permitForm`, alternating, rotating over the axis bits |
| `builder` | `fundUniversity` slot 0 (found) every 40th tick, slot 1 (advance the least finished) otherwise |
| `canon` | a four-phase rotation of permit-technique, permit-form, fund-university, bless-mage |

This is a single seed and four hand-written policies. It is a diagnostic, not a baseline: it is
sufficient to show that a quantity does or does not move with play, and insufficient to fix a
constant. Every constant proposed here is `untuned` and the sweep that tunes it is named in the
claim.

### At world tick 2400

| policy | worship | tier | nodes known | libDep | goodEraRun | completed universities | favor discarded |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `passive` | 4,636 | 4 | 51 | 0% | 9 | 1 | 9,530,689 |
| `breadth` | 4,994 | 4 | 182 | 3% | 5 | 1 | 9,119,100 |
| `builder` | 8,503 | **5** | 51 | 0% | 9 | 60 | 12,353,708 |
| `canon` | 8,507 | **5** | 174 | 2% | **0** | 60 | 9,622,917 |

### The four facts the design rests on

1. **Worship tier 4 is the passive ceiling and tier 5 is not.** `passive` crosses tier 4 at tick
   720 and asymptotes at 4,636 by 2400, never approaching the 8,192 threshold. `builder` — one
   university every 40 ticks — reaches 8,503 and crosses tier 5 between ticks 1800 and 2400. The
   mechanism is not only the university worship class: universities carry capacity, capacity carries
   mages, and `builder`'s living-mage count reaches 955 against `passive`'s 72, so the mage class
   saturates too. Nothing but the god founds a university (`coordination/src/world-step.ts` says so
   in as many words, and `advanceConstruction` has no caller outside `fundUniversity`), which is
   what makes the whole of tier 5 god-attributable.
2. **Path A opened passively at tick 709; Path B opened passively at tick 1080.** `ascensionPath`
   flips to `apotheosis` at 709 with zero submissions. `goodEraRun` reaches 4 at 1080 and 9 by 2400.
   Fixing Path A alone would leave the idle strategy winning through Path B at tick 1080.
3. **Path B's sign is inverted.** The passive universe measures `libraryDependence` **0%** at every
   sampled tick from 360 onward and loses **0** nodes per era; `canon` — broad, populous, well
   funded — fails, and the boundary probe says which conjunct fires: at the boundary near tick 960
   it carried `eraNodesLost = 7` against `ascension-loss-max = 2`, with dependence at 12% and
   passing. A 51-node canon in stasis is the easiest thing in the game to keep intact.
4. **Every policy sits at its favor cap.** Discarded regeneration over 2400 ticks ranges from 9.1M
   to 12.4M fp; regeneration is roughly 3,000–4,000 fp per tick at these worship levels against a
   cap of 61,440–71,680. A favor price is therefore *cheapest for the idler*, who has spent nothing.

## Goals / Non-Goals

**Goals**

- Make both qualifying paths read something the god's play moves.
- Keep the authored retune order intact, and say exactly where any new knob sits relative to it.
- Make the discrimination property itself a gate, not an argument.

**Non-Goals**

- Adding ascension routes. That is W3, and it is blocked on this change.
- Touching `packages/mc-harness/src/strategies.ts` or the sweep instrument. That is W1.
- Retuning the worship economy. `worship-university-half` and its neighbours are `worshipSnowball`'s
  knobs and carry the 0.8.0 claim.
- Tuning any constant to a final value. The values here are placeholders with a stated direction.

## Decisions

### D1 — Path A: raise `ascension-tier-gate` from 4 to 5, and change no rule

The authored order says this knob turns first, and the measurement says it works. It is the rare
case where the coarse knob is also the right one, because of *which* quantity it is coarse over:
the three worship classes saturate at 4,096 (mages), 3,072 (universities) and 2,048 (populace), and
the university class is the only one the god creates from nothing. A universe that never acts cannot
buy the last 3,500 points of worship at any population.

The knob has exactly two usable settings and this is worth stating plainly: worship tiers are
geometric (512, 1024, 2048, 4096, 8192) and the passive universe sits at 4,636 — comfortably over
the tier-4 threshold and half of the tier-5 one. There is no setting between "opens passively" and
"requires a building programme". After this change the gate stands at `worship-tier-count`'s
ceiling, so Path A's first knob is spent; the successor is D5's rejected alternative, and it should
be reconsidered only if the correlation gate fails on Path A specifically.

**Cost of the requirement, in play:** a university costs `found-university-cost` fp 10,240 plus four
`fund-university` actions at fp 3,072, i.e. fp 22,528 and five actions. At the measured regeneration
rate that is about six ticks of income — affordable, and 60 of them is a programme rather than a
purchase. The point is not that it is expensive. The point is that it cannot happen by accident.

### D2 — Path B: add a breadth conjunct and make the loss allowance scale

Path B cannot be fixed by any of its three authored knobs, and this is a proof rather than a
preference:

- `ascension-dependence-max` (fp 256 = 25%) cannot exclude a universe that measures **0%**. No
  positive setting does.
- `ascension-era-count` (4) only delays: the passive universe reaches `goodEraRun` 9 within the
  2400-tick horizon, so a setting that excludes it (≥ 10) also puts the path past `MC_MAX_TICKS`
  and kills it. Trading a passive win for a dead path is the failure D1 was careful to avoid.
- `ascension-loss-max` (2) is already the conjunct that fires — against the *active* god.

So Path B needs a rule change, and the smallest honest one has two parts.

**A canon has a size.** A passing era boundary requires `nodesKnown ≥ ascension-canon-breadth`.
Vision §8a's second summit is *"a civilization that has held its knowledge intact across enough
eras"*, and holding 51 nodes that nobody touches is not what that sentence is about. Breadth is
also the axis F3 found to be the whole strategy space, and it is a god axis: the ruleset toggles are
vision pillar 1, and `permissive-breadth` reaches 273 nodes where the passive control reaches 51.
Placeholder 96 — a little under twice the passive baseline, a little over half what a naive breadth
policy reaches by tick 1200.

**A loss cap over a scaling quantity measures scale.** The allowance becomes
`max(ascension-loss-max, floorDiv(nodesKnown × ascension-loss-fraction, fp(1024)))`. At 51 nodes it
is 2, exactly what is authored today; at 174 nodes it is 8, against the 7 that disqualified `canon`.
`ascension-loss-max` is retained as the floor so that a small canon is not handed a free loss by the
fraction rounding to zero, and so the authored constant keeps meaning what it meant.

**Walking the measured boundaries through the proposed predicate.** The `canon` policy's era
boundaries were probed directly (`eraNodesLost` and `nodesKnown` at the tick before each boundary):

| boundary near tick | nodes known | lost in era | allowance under the proposal | passes? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 240 | 72 | 0 | 2 | no — 72 < 96 breadth |
| 480 | 139 | 2 | 6 | yes |
| 720 | 157 | 7 | 7 | yes |
| 960 | 162 | 7 | 8 | yes (fails today at 2) |
| 1200 | 169 | 0 | 8 | yes |

`goodEraRun` would therefore reach 4 at about tick 1200 and Path B would qualify for a god that
permits, builds and blesses — comfortably inside the 2400-tick horizon — while the passive universe,
flat at 51 nodes, never passes a boundary at all. That is the shape D1 was careful to preserve for
Path A: the fix must make the path *harder for the idler*, not closed for everyone.

The two parts must land together. Breadth alone would make Path B unwinnable — the measurement is
unambiguous that a broad universe loses more than two nodes per era — and the scaled allowance alone
would leave the passive universe passing every boundary.

### D3 — Price `declare-ascension` at fp 20,480, bounded by `favor-cap-base`

The declaration is free today, and the gloss defending that is *"charging for the ending would make
a god who spent well unable to stop."* That is a real hazard and the answer is arithmetic rather
than zero: favor regenerates, so a price is a delay and not a lockout, and pinning the price at or
below `favor-cap-base` (fp 20,480, the pool ceiling at worship tier 0) makes the delay bounded at
*every* tier, including for a Path B universe that qualifies at tier 1. That relationship is a
loader invariant, in the style `prestige-cap`'s identity already uses: a retune that breaks it fails
the load rather than producing a universe that qualifies and cannot end.

What the price is for: stopping should trade against playing. Fp 20,480 is ten blessings, two
university foundings, twenty encouragements, or about six ticks of banked regeneration. It gives the
terminal reward a gradient, which is the property a learned agent needs and a free button denies.

What the price is *not* for: discrimination. Measured, every policy sits at its cap for most of the
run, and the idler has the fullest pool of all. A price is regressive with respect to exactly the
property this change is about, and it would be dishonest to present it as the fix.

### D4 — Add a correlation gate beside the band

`ascensionRate` was 0.125 — inside 5–20% — while one strategy won every run. A band over an
aggregate cannot see the failure, so the property gets its own gate: on W1's long-horizon sweep,
`passive-control`'s ascension rate must sit below the pool mean and at least two other strategies
must sit above it, both by more than the reported standard error. This is asserted against a
per-strategy reading, alongside the existing dead-path check (`ascensionRateByPath` over 90% on one
path).

### D5 — Alternatives rejected

1. **Gate on cumulative favor spent** (the dual of `favorWasted`, which the god-state row already
   carries). Rejected: `assign-role` costs fp 256, so any threshold is reachable by spamming the
   cheapest action — 80 presses buys fp 20,480 of "investment". It measures submissions, not play,
   and it is the button-press meta transposed into a counter.
2. **Require the summit mage to be blessed at declaration.** Rejected: fp 2,048, one action,
   refreshable, and it turns "press one button" into "press two". It reads as investment and is not
   one.
3. **Add `completedUniversities ≥ N` as a Path A conjunct** instead of raising the tier gate.
   Rejected as redundant: universities are already the only worship class the god creates, so the
   tier gate discriminates *through* the same fact, and the authored retune order prescribes the
   tier gate first. It is also weaker on observability — §4.1's institutions block carries the
   university *count*, not how many are complete, so a policy could only bound the conjunct from
   above. Named here because it is the successor knob if D1's ceiling is ever reached.
4. **Lower `ascension-dependence-max`.** Rejected: the passive universe measures 0%.
5. **Raise `ascension-era-count`.** Rejected: delays without discriminating, and any excluding
   setting exceeds the horizon.
6. **Raise `ascension-min-tick`.** Rejected: a floor against rushes, applied equally to everyone. It
   moves the rate and not the correlation.
7. **Retune the worship class constants** so universities count for more. Rejected: those are
   `worshipSnowball`'s knobs and carry the 0.8.0 release claim; two gates on one knob means the next
   sweep cannot say which claim moved.

## Risks / Trade-offs

- **Path A gets late.** A moderate builder crosses tier 5 between ticks 1800 and 2400, against a
  `MC_MAX_TICKS` of 2400, so Path A's contribution to `ascensionRate` will fall and may fall out of
  the band. That is what the authored order is for: the band violation is answered by the knobs in
  their order, and this change deliberately does not pre-emptively compensate.
- **Path A's knob is spent** at `worship-tier-count`'s ceiling. Recorded in the proposal as an
  accepted risk with a named successor.
- **`ascension-canon-breadth` is content-coupled.** 96 nodes is a fraction of a 300-node graph with
  twelve enabled cells; content that enables more cells moves what the constant means. It is
  deliberately a count and not a fraction of the graph, because a fraction would make the summit
  recede as content grows — but it must be re-read whenever cells are enabled, and the tasks say so.
- **One seed, four policies.** The diagnostic establishes direction, not magnitude. Every number
  here is `untuned`.
- **`contentRevision` moves and a golden fixture may move with it.** Not regenerated; escalated.

## Where the measurement disagrees with vision §8a

Both are reported rather than resolved, because the vision is the document of record and this change
has no authority to amend it.

1. **"Reachable but not routine" is a statement about an aggregate, and an aggregate can hide a
   strategy for which it is routine.** Measured: `ascensionRate` 0.125, inside the band, while
   `uniform-random-legal` ascends 10 of 10. The constraint that was actually wanted is per-strategy,
   and D4 asserts it there. §8a's wording should be read as necessary and not sufficient.
2. **"A civilization that has held its knowledge intact across enough eras" currently describes the
   null hypothesis.** In this build, holding knowledge intact is what a universe does when nobody
   touches it: zero losses, zero library dependence, nine good eras. Vision §8a's second summit is
   sound as intent and, as implemented, is a description of `passive-control`. D2 is the smallest
   change that makes the sentence mean what it says; if the vision would rather that Path B reward
   *stability* per se, then Path B is the wrong shape for a discriminating win condition and W3
   should give custodianship its own route with a different score.

## Migration Plan

Content and rules land together — the constants are meaningless without the predicate and the
predicate is unreachable without the constants. `contentRevision` moves once. The correlation gate
is added to the harness in the same change but asserted only against W1's long-horizon sweep, which
must exist first; the tasks make that a precondition rather than a hope.

## Open Questions

- Does any committed golden fixture's action log reach an era boundary or declare ascension? If one
  does, its simulated result moves and the campaign's no-regeneration rule turns that into an
  escalation.
- Should `recordEra` retain `nodesKnown` alongside the dependence and loss it already stores? It
  would make a failed boundary self-explanatory in a sweep record, at the cost of a component field.
- Does the observation need a completed-university channel? Not for this change — the mask carries
  the truth about eligibility — but D5's successor knob would need one, and adding a channel is a
  §4.1 layout change with a baseline-invalidating digest.
