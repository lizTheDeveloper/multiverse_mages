Multiverse Mages

You decide what magic may exist. The academics handle the consequences.

Set the permitted techniques and forms of magic for your universe. You do not appoint professors, direct research, or tell anyone to stop pursuing decorative applications of structural fire. You establish the ruleset. The population will interpret it with the seriousness normally reserved for tenure.

Mages discover spells, teach apprentices, copy grimoires, stock libraries, and develop local traditions. Different species pursue different parts of the lattice, with different lifespans and different standards for what counts as sufficiently proven. Then portals open, and neighboring universes submit their curricula for review.

On the disposal of knowledge

Knowledge belongs to particular people and particular places. A mage may carry a spell only in their head. A library may preserve it until a shelf is lost. When mastery decays, teaching stops. When the last holder dies, the universe has completed a permanent administrative correction.

Consequences
    Real content: technique.json, form.json, cell.json, node.json — 5 techniques, 14 forms, 70 cells, 300 authored nodes.
    Legality is vision §4's rule: a cell functions iff its technique and form are both permitted, after edicts. Reachability additionally requires every prerequisite to be reachable, because a dormant instance cannot satisfy one — which is why forbidding a technique costs more nodes than it holds.
    Audio is synthesised live (sound-design §0.8, synthesised tier); no recorded assets are loaded. This page reimplements permits() rather than importing it — a stated, temporary exception to contracts.md §5.
