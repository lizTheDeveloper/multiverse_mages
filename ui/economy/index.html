The economy

Who produces, who consumes, and what is short — and why there is no allocation control on this page.

The stocks three kinds, no substitution between them

A shortfall in one kind is a shortfall. There is no market and no debt: a claimant that cannot be paid gets what is there and the rest is recorded as short (materials.ts:58-61, 287-317). Letting a hungry universe eat its quarry would be a mechanic nobody asked for.

The whole run

Every frame this page holds, at a shared scale within each row. These are stocks over time, not production: the read path drops per-tick flows, so the slope of a line is production minus consumption and this page will not claim to know which half moved. Hover any facet to read every series at one tick.

 

The three trades below are drawn against their own, much smaller scale — put them on the row above and all three flatten onto the baseline, which is true and hides the shape of the decline. Each axis prints the maximum it was drawn against.

Who produces a formula over population, not over entities

There is no farm and no quarry. Field production is laborers × 16 × the species' labour affinity, split across the three kinds by this universe's territory yield shares (materials.ts:86, 164-174). A laborer standing on a building site is excluded from the fields that month — labour is exclusive, and letting her be both would make founding a university free (world-step.ts:1240). Mages casting apply-magic produce too, routed to a kind by the node's form (kinds.ts:172).

Who consumes five claimants, in a fixed order

Walked in this order every tick (materials.ts:194-202). Only the vellum pair still competes — library upkeep is paid before scribing out of the same stock, because losing a shelved instance is permanent in a way a delayed grimoire is not. The consequence of running short differs per kind, and that is the most useful thing on this page.
