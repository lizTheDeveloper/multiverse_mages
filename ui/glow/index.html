The god's light

One rule: cyan is the god — potential, affordance, and the moment of touch. The fourteen form hues stay the colour of magic that exists in the world, and nothing glows that the god cannot currently do, so absence of light is the legality mask rather than a red warning.

Two themes, not three grounds. Vellum is light and ink is dark; the hybrid this page used to compare was answering a question nobody asked. Switch the theme and watch the charged state invert — on ink it emits light, on paper it blooms downward.

What this settles

The same token means opposite things in the two themes, and that is the point. On ink, charge is light emitted from the control. On vellum it cannot be — a cyan fill bright enough to read measured 1.01 : 1 against latent, a hue difference with no luminance at all, invisible to a colourblind player and on any poor panel. So on paper charge blooms downward: ink drawn to the control rather than light coming off it. Both resolve from --ctl-charged-* and --glow-charged, so a prototype writes one rule and gets the right treatment in either theme. If component CSS ever has to ask which theme it is in, the theme layer has failed.

Why absence rather than red. sound-design §2.2 makes the deny sound the legality mask made audible. Giving deny its own colour would make the interface shout on every illegal click, and an RL agent submits illegal actions constantly. Unlit and dashed says the same thing quietly — but the label stays at full strength, because dimming it with opacity measured 2.42 : 1 on dark, under WCAG's 4.5 for body text. The state was legible only to someone who could already see it.

The collision to watch. Cyan is not one of the fourteen form hues and must never become one. Aquam is the risk — the obvious hue for water is the hue chosen here for the god. Aquam is outside the v1 subset, so the collision is not live yet, which is exactly why it should be settled now. sound-design §1.3 is the precedent: it allocates a frequency band per subsystem and treats that allocation as a contract rather than a palette. Hue is the same shape one dimension over, and the fourteen forms plus the god are the entire budget.

Prototype · not the client. Controls are inert demonstrations of state; no content is loaded and no rule is evaluated — ui/ruleset/ is where legality actually runs. Tokens live in ui/shared/theme.css.
