The god's light

One rule: cyan is the god — potential, affordance, and the moment of touch. The fourteen form hues stay the colour of magic that exists in the world, and nothing glows that the god cannot currently do, so absence of light is the legality mask rather than a red warning.

Glow wants a dark ground. art-plan §2 specifies vellum. Below is the same set of controls on three grounds, so the trade can be looked at instead of argued about. Every control is live — click the charged ones.

What this is for

The collision to watch. Cyan is not one of the fourteen form hues, and it must never become one. Aquam is the risk — the obvious hue for water is the hue chosen here for the god. Aquam is outside the v1 subset, so the collision is not live yet, and that is exactly why it should be written down now rather than discovered when the eighth form gets its colour.

Why absence rather than red. sound-design §2.2 makes the deny sound the legality mask made audible. Giving deny its own colour would make the interface shout on every illegal click, and an RL agent submits illegal actions constantly. Unlit and dashed says the same thing quietly, and keeps the one saturated register for things that are actually happening.

Why the touch decays. A steady glow on a spent intervention would read as "still active". Interventions resolve on the tick they are issued; the light should behave the same way.

Prototype · not the client. Controls are inert demonstrations of state; no content is loaded and no rule is evaluated — ui/ruleset/ is where legality actually runs.
