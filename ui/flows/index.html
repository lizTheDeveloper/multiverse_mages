The flow map

Sources, stocks, sinks — for all seven material kinds. Two maps kept apart: what can flow, which is a fact about the content and holds for every run, and what this run's stocks did, which is a fact about one seed. What flowed per tick is neither, and the last panel says why.

Three columns: what makes material, the seven stocks it lands in, and every claimant that can take it away. Follow one kind with the chips above and the map draws only that kind's plumbing. What running out actually does the reason the seven are not one number

There is no debt, no market, and no substitution between kinds — a shortfall clamps at zero and is recorded as a shortfall. What differs, and what a player most needs the map to teach, is the consequence. One of these is not recoverable.

The measured lanes what this run's stocks did · fed, per kind

One lane per kind, in MATERIAL_KINDS order, the same order as the map above. Each carries the stock over the whole run, the consequence series beside it, and — where the god has a verb priced in that kind — a dashed line at the price and a strip saying whether the verb was legal. The crossing and the strip are independent readings: the stock comes from the stocks sidecar and the strip from the §4.2 mask, and when they coincide to the tick that is a measurement, not a drawing.

The measured map what did flow, per tick · from the flow ledger

The structural map above says what can flow. This says what did, summed over the run, from the per-tick flow sidecar — the only reading on this page that is a flow rather than a level. Every other number here is a stock or a difference of stocks, and a difference of stocks nets a faucet against a sink. This does not.
